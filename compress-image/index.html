Compress Image

Reduce the file size of JPEG, PNG or WebP images with an adjustable quality slider. Processed entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded.

compress
Drop images here or click to choose
JPEG, PNG or WebP · multiple files supported

    PNG is a lossless format, so the quality slider won't shrink it much. For a bigger reduction, switch the output format to WebP or JPEG.

    How it works

    1. Add one or more JPEG, PNG or WebP images.
    2. Pick a quality level and, optionally, a different output format.
    3. Click "Compress Images" and download each result individually.
