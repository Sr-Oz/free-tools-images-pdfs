How to Rotate, Delete and Reorder PDF Pages

Published 14 August 2026 · 3 min read

Every scanner app produces the occasional sideways page, blank cover sheet, or pages saved in the wrong order. None of that requires re-scanning the whole document — a page-level editor is enough to fix it in place.

Step by step

  1. Open Organise PDF Pages and add your file — every page renders as a thumbnail.
  2. To reorder: drag a thumbnail to its new position.
  3. To rotate: use the ⟲ / ⟳ buttons on a page — each click turns it 90°.
  4. To remove: click ✕ to mark a page for deletion (click again to bring it back if you change your mind before saving).
  5. Click "Save PDF" once everything looks right.

A few practical tips

  • Nothing is final until you save. Rotations and deletions are just marks on the working copy — you can undo a deletion by clicking ✕ again, right up until you click "Save PDF."
  • Only need to pull a few pages out, not reorganize the rest? Split PDF is faster for that — pick the pages you want and extract them directly.
  • Rotation is per-page, not global. If half your scan is sideways and half isn't, that's fine — rotate only the pages that need it.
