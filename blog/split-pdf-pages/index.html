How to Split a PDF Into Separate Pages

Published 14 August 2026 · 3 min read

Sometimes you don't want a whole PDF — you want page 4, or pages 10 through 15, or you need to break a 40-page scan into one file per page for an upload form that only accepts single pages. Splitting a PDF is quick once you can see the pages you're working with.

Step by step

  1. Open the Split PDF tool and add your file.
  2. Every page renders as a thumbnail so you can see what you're selecting.
  3. Click pages to select them, or type a range like 1-3,5,8-10 into the text field — clicking and typing stay in sync.
  4. Choose "Extract Selected Pages" for one combined file, or "Download Each Selected Page Separately" if you need individual files.

A few practical tips

  • Downloading many separate files will prompt your browser. Most browsers ask permission before allowing several downloads at once — that's your browser protecting you from download spam, not a bug in the tool.
  • Selecting nothing selects nothing. If you want the whole document minus one page, it's often faster to use "Select all" then click the one page to deselect it.
  • Thumbnails are rendered locally. The preview images are generated in your browser using pdf.js — the file is never sent anywhere just to show you what's in it.
