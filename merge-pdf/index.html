Merge PDF

Combine multiple PDFs into one file, in any order you like. Processed locally — nothing is uploaded.

📎
Drop PDF files here or click to choose
Two or more PDFs · use the arrows to reorder

    How it works

    1. Add two or more PDF files.
    2. Reorder them with the ↑ / ↓ buttons if needed.
    3. Click "Merge PDFs" to download the combined file.
